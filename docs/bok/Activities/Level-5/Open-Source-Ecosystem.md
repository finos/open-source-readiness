Use open source as an ecosystem strategy: attract contributors, partners, and customers to a shared platform.

## When to use this play

- Your roadmap depends on adoption beyond your own organisation.
- You want partners to extend a platform rather than wait for proprietary integrations.
- You need market validation and faster innovation from external users.

## What to do

1. Publish clear contribution guidance, governance, and technical direction.
2. Invest in onboarding, documentation, and developer experience.
3. Build a repeatable community rhythm (issues, roadmaps, releases, and communication).
4. Align product and OSPO leadership on where openness should accelerate growth.

## Success signals

- More external contributors and maintainers.
- More ecosystem integrations and reference implementations.
- Reduced internal delivery burden through shared development.
