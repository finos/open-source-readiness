Use open source participation to shape enterprise strategy, not just execute engineering tasks.

## When to use this play

- Leadership decisions depend on ecosystem direction and standards.
- Build-vs-buy choices are constrained by vendor lock-in or roadmap misalignment.
- You need an OSPO voice in executive technology planning.

## What to do

1. Include OSPO input in architecture, vendor, and platform decision forums.
2. Track strategic dependencies in key upstream projects and foundations.
3. Contribute deliberately where influence changes your long-term options.
4. Tie open source actions to business outcomes and report them to executives.

## Success signals

- Open source criteria appear in core technology decision processes.
- Upstream influence reduces downstream rework and technical debt.
- Leadership views OSPO as a strategic partner, not only an operational function.
