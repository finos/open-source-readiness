OSMM Level 5 is about [Strategy](../../Artifacts/Strategy). At this level, strategy should be action-oriented and expressed as a set of practical plays.

Use this page as a playbook index. Each play below is intentionally concise and can be applied independently depending on your goals.

## Core Strategic Plays

1. [Commodification of Complements](Commodification-Of-Complements): reduce dependency and vendor leverage by open-sourcing non-differentiating layers around your strategic products.
2. [Open Source Ecosystem](Open-Source-Ecosystem): build a partner and contributor ecosystem that increases adoption and influence.
3. [Open-Source-Led Strategy](Open-Source-Led-Strategy): use open source participation to directly shape technology choices, standards, and market direction.
4. [Why Open-Source a Firm Project?](Why-To-Create-A-Project): connect project creation to business outcomes such as hiring, marketing, and strategic positioning.

## How to use these plays

- Start with your strategic objective (cost leverage, adoption, talent, standards influence, or ecosystem growth).
- Select one primary play and one supporting play.
- Define measurable outcomes and review progress with leadership on a regular cadence.
