## Types of Intellectual Property

### Trademarks

Protects symbols, names, and slogans used to identify goods or services.

### Copyright

Protects original works of authorship, such as writings, music, and art.

### Patents

Protects inventions or discoveries for a limited period, granting the inventor exclusive rights to the invention.

### Trade Secrets

Protects confidential business information that provides a competitive edge.

## Information Classification

Information classification is closely related to intellectual property because it determines whether material can be treated as public, kept internal, or restricted to a smaller audience. This matters directly when deciding whether code, documentation, or related materials are safe to publish to open source repositories.

For more detail, see [Information Classification](Information-Classification).
