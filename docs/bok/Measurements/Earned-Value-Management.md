Earned Value Management (EVM) is a way to measure delivery progress by comparing:

- **Planned Value (PV):** what you expected to deliver by a point in time.
- **Earned Value (EV):** the value of what has actually been delivered.
- **Actual Cost (AC):** the effort or spend used to deliver it.

For Open Source Readiness (OSR), EVM can be applied to activity plans such as policy rollout, onboarding teams, contribution enablement, and supply-chain controls.

## What To Measure

<BoxOut title="OSR Delivery Performance" image="/img/bok/metric.png">

Define a fixed time-box (for example a quarter), then estimate and track:

- **PV:** planned completion of OSR activities from your roadmap.
- **EV:** completed OSR outcomes (for example approved policy, staffed OSPO support process, or teams trained and contributing).
- **AC:** delivery cost, such as internal effort, tooling spend, and external support.

From these values, calculate:

- **Schedule Variance (SV) = EV - PV** (positive is ahead of plan).
- **Cost Variance (CV) = EV - AC** (positive is under budget).
- **Schedule Performance Index (SPI) = EV / PV**.
- **Cost Performance Index (CPI) = EV / AC**.

</BoxOut>

## Why This Helps

Using EVM for OSR helps leadership see whether readiness investments are producing measurable outcomes, and whether the programme is tracking to plan in both time and cost.
