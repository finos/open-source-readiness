This section explores legal and compliance considerations that may affect the adoption and operation of InnerSource in financial services organisations.

## Regulatory Boundaries and InnerSource

Financial services organisations often operate across multiple legal entities, regulated businesses, jurisdictions and governance structures. Certain regulations may require parts of an organisation to maintain operational independence from others.

When InnerSource collaboration crosses these boundaries, teams need to consider whether the way a project is consumed, supported or maintained could create prohibited service relationships or operational dependencies.

This does not necessarily prevent collaboration. However, it can affect how collaboration must be structured and where ownership and accountability should remain.
